Why Camploop exists

Built for hostel rooms,
not warehouses.

Every semester, students buy new and let perfectly good things pile up in a corner when they leave. Camploop exists so that pile becomes someone else's find — without ever leaving campus.

🎒 One account, two roles

There's no separate "seller dashboard" to apply for. Every student can list a product the moment they sign up, and browse the marketplace the very next second.

♻️ Reuse over rebuy

Textbooks, calculators, cycles and hostel gear get handed down instead of thrown out — cheaper for the buyer, less clutter for the seller.

🤝 Student to student

You're buying from someone in your own college — not an anonymous warehouse. Contact them directly and arrange the handoff wherever's convenient on campus.

🔒 Designed around trust

Real listings, real students, real campus. V1 keeps it simple and honest — future versions will add ID verification and ratings.

How it works

Discover → Connect → Exchange

Three steps, no middleman, no shipping label.

STEP 1

🔍 Find a product you need

Browse or search the marketplace by category, price, or condition until something clicks.

STEP 2

💬 Connect with the seller

Every listing shows the seller's name so you can reach out and ask questions directly.

STEP 3

🤝 Exchange on campus

Agree on a spot, meet up, and hand it off. Or list your own item just as easily.
